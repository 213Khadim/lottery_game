Responsible Play

A lottery ticket is entertainment, not a way to earn money. We give you tools to stay in control from your first day.

18+ Age verified with CNIC
Spending limits

Daily, weekly and monthly caps with alerts at 80%.

Take a break

Pause deposits and paid tickets for 24 hours, 7 or 30 days.

Self-exclusion

Block paid play for 6 months to 5 years. It can't be undone early.

Reality checks

Regular reminders of time and money spent, plus monthly statements.

Send money by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer to the account shown, then submit the amount, reference number and a screenshot. Our finance team checks it, usually within a few hours, and the status changes from Pending to Approved or Rejected.

After entries close, a random draw uses a server seed (its fingerprint is published before the draw) plus a public seed. After the draw we publish the server seed, so anyone can re-run it and get the same winners. The draw is shown live in the app.
